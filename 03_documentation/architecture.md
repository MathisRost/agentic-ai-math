# Agent architecture

## Research experiment, 20 October 2026

A coordinator first dispatched two independent obstruction investigators. Fresh reviewers audited their frozen submissions before S0 was replaced by the diameter-capped S1. Four construction tracks then investigated Poisson clusters, Cox processes, Hawkes/branching processes and an investigator-selected method. Each track had a four-submission allowance; the Cox track closed early with owner approval. Approach 4 reached the reviewed S1 construction on its fourth submission.

## Paper and simulation, 21 October 2026

Exactly three delegated agents worked with the coordinator, all verified as `gpt-6-astra/xhigh`:

| Role | Responsibility | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Writer | Mathematical exposition in the requested order and style | [Frozen drafts](../experiments/reciprocal-simplex-astra6/02_writing_record/writer/) |
| Coder | Exact small-ball sampler, weighted correlation estimates, figures and derivations | [Simulation supplement](../experiments/reciprocal-simplex-astra6/01_simulation/README.md) |
| Reader | Fresh reading of the whole frozen integrated manuscript and both figures | [Independent report](../experiments/reciprocal-simplex-astra6/02_writing_record/reader/review_v01.md) |

The coordinator integrated the work and addressed four reader findings. The reader received the manuscript, figures, prerequisites and scope, without author conversations or desired findings. These were cooperation rules on a shared filesystem, not operating-system isolation. The final local clarification/layout repairs were checked by the coordinator, not reread independently.
