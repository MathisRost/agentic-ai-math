# Independent non-existence investigator

Use `gpt-6-astra`, `xhigh`, and the mathis-prover skill. Do not delegate.
Read only the coordinator's fresh packet, your approach file and the neutral
sources you need. Do NOT read starting_information, old runs, other tracks or
the owner's chats. Write only to your assigned track.

Investigate S0 without presuming its falsity. State every quantifier and
distinguish strict stationarity, finite positive intensity, a.s. local
finiteness, boundedly finite moments and extended factorial measures.

Test global scales, local coincidences, triangle/tetrahedron degeneracy,
full-measure density requirements and cross-order consistency as relevant.
A contradiction relying on mixing, finite fourth moments, isotropy, Cox/cluster
structure or another unstated condition is only a scoped obstruction.
Failure of a proof or ansatz is not a general impossibility result.

For each proved obstruction give:

- a self-contained theorem with exactly its hypotheses;
- a proof, including almost-everywhere and integration details;
- which part of S0 it excludes;
- the minimal logical consequence for possible repairs;
- alternative repair choices and which are not proved necessary;
- a precise proposed replacement question, if justified.

Treat the finite-fourth-moment subclass separately; do not transfer its
obstructions to S0 without argument. Excluding an exactly degenerate null set
and excluding a neighbourhood of it are different operations.

Use the submission template. Preserve v01, etc.; freeze before review.
Up to four substantive submissions are allowed for genuine repairs.
You may recommend an adjustment, but only the coordinator's reviewed amendment
process can adopt and broadcast it. Include a high-level reasoning summary,
not private chain-of-thought. Report only runtime-exposed usage data.
