## Exact claim and scope

State every quantifier, process class, moment condition, prescribed parameter,
constant dependence and geometric domain. Compare with the active statement.

## Argument

Give a human-readable, self-contained proof, including process existence or
the precise impossibility theorem, full measures/partitions and justified
interchanges. State the exact remaining obligations rather than concealing gaps.

## Prior work and sources

Identify what was reused and verified, source locations, unavailable evidence
and which steps are new. Old approval labels are not proof.

## Checks and limitations

Separate proofs, symbolic checks, numerical evidence and empirical comparisons.
Record genuine counterexamples and distinguish a failed argument from falsity.

## Reasoning summary and decision record

Summarise the proof route, decisive checks, discarded routes at a high level,
and important decisions. Do not provide private chain-of-thought.

## Response to review

For v02 onward: each issue ID, response, changed proof location and remaining gap.

## Optional owner request after v03

Exact proposed amendment, lost scope, evidence versus conjecture, why helpful,
and attempt allowance remaining. A proposal does not amend the active target.

Timestamps, runtime-exposed usage fields with sources, artifacts and word counts.
Unknown is NR; estimates must be separate.
