random directing measure. Prove \(\alpha_X^{(n)}=\mathbb E\Lambda^{\otimes n}\)
with the needed extended-measure interpretation; do not assume densities exist
merely because the directing measure exists.

but unconditional full measures must have the target densities with no
overlooked singular contribution. Include all latent-carrier allocations.
Moment positivity and consistency must concern ONE random measure.

general Cox non-existence. Infinite fourth moments are not forbidden unless the
active target says so. A cumulant formula or arbitrary approximation does not
prove the full exact identity. Check the shared seed's scope independently.
