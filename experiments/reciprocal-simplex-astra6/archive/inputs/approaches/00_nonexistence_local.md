# Non-existence 2 — local geometry and moment classes

Independently investigate the complete S0. Give particular attention to
coincidences, collinearity, coplanarity, singular components, measure
integrability and consistency of the full hierarchy.

Keep separate the broad finite-intensity, a.s. locally finite class and the
finite-fourth-local-moment subclass. A divergent expected fourth count is not
by itself a failure of almost-sure local finiteness. Conversely a purported
density must be the full expectation measure, not only its absolutely
continuous component.

For any obstruction identify the precise assumptions used. Consider whether
it forces a cutoff, a different domain, a different moment class, or something
else; do not equate one convenient repair with a necessary condition.
Do not read old proofs or the other investigator. Check S0 beyond this emphasis
when needed. Your final result may be an honest non-result.
