# Construction 3 — Hawkes/branching processes

Define the model producing a point process on R^3 before calculating densities.
A temporal Hawkes process alone is not the required spatial process. If using
space-time projection, specify a finite observation interval, prove spatial
stationarity/local finiteness/simplicity and match its intensity to lambda_0.
Projecting all time may create infinite counts.

Derive spatial FULL densities with all genealogies and immigrant partitions;
an excitation kernel, resolvent or cumulant is not a product density.
Justify interchanges and the moment/existence assumptions for the actual
subcritical, critical or heavy-tailed regime used. Do not borrow a theorem
from another regime without checking its hypotheses.

Meet the adopted exact target, not merely earthquake plausibility. If the law
leaves the Hawkes class, say so. Record a proved family-specific obstruction
without generalising it to all stationary processes. The four-attempt rule and
owner checkpoint apply.
