# Non-existence 1 — global compatibility

Independently investigate the complete S0. Give particular attention to
stationarity, positive finite intensity, arbitrarily large scales and the
constraints on full factorial measures. Do not impose mixing or convergence
to Poisson behaviour without proving that it follows from the stated class.
Use bounded-set and occupancy arguments if useful, but choose your own proof.

If a general impossibility is proved, state exactly the range of scales/shapes
it excludes. Separate necessity of SOME modification from necessity of a
particular finite diameter cutoff. Propose the broadest defensible replacement
question, with every intensity and geometric quantifier explicit.

Do not read prior proof packets or the other investigator. A result in a
particular process family is not a general obstruction. Review the complete
target, not merely your preferred emphasis.
