# Unresolved obligations — v01

S1 remains unchanged. The inspected sources do not settle existence or nonexistence.

1. Can a real-valued positive functional on a sufficiently rich class of integrable fourth-order tests be established from the prescribed measures? Nonnegativity of each density is insufficient.
2. Can a regularity modulus and bound satisfying LM2015 Theorem 2.6 be verified for that class without imposing a finite fourth local moment? Merely asserting tightness does not answer this.
3. Would equality on the selected tests identify the entire sigma-finite fourth measure, including zero mass on omitted null/degenerate sets and absolute continuity outside the prescribed local domain?
4. Can lower moments, stationary extension, simplicity and compatible local laws be retained simultaneously? These are outstanding application steps, not conclusions from the sources.
5. Is there a primary theorem tailored directly to non-Radon sigma-finite factorial measures? This bounded search did not locate and verify one. Lenard's original 1975 papers were not inspected; references to them are not substitutes for their hypotheses. No extension is requested merely to turn non-discovery into a broader negative claim.

A useful next source request would identify a specific claimed theorem handling extended factorial measures or a specific proposed restricted test space. It should ask for that exact bridge, not a general survey. No unproved bridge has been filled here.
