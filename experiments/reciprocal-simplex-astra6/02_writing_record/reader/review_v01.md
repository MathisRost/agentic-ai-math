What I understood from the supplied text:

- **Claim — stated explicitly.** For each independently fixed positive intensity lambda and diameter M, there exists a stationary, simple, locally finite process whose full factorial measures through order four have global densities finite almost everywhere. On the nondegenerate diameter-capped domains these equal a_n/Delta_n. The positive coefficients are chosen by the construction. Third count moments are finite, fourth count moments in open balls are infinite, and the law is nonergodic (Section 3, Theorem `thm:main`, lines 256–277). The abstract matches this scope. Section 2 explains both ordered tuples and the difference between an extended measure and a locally integrable density.
- **Coin mechanism — stated and recoverable from conditioning.** One fair coin selects an entire sphere process or an entire correction process, so factorial measures average without cross-component tuples (Section 2, `eq:mixture-measure`, lines 224–235; Section 4, `eq:fair-coin`, lines 317–327). Surface separation makes every eligible tuple a same-sphere tuple. The radius weight gives the fourth reciprocal volume exactly. The other alternative supplies the lower-order deficits through separated clusters of at most three points. Component intensities average to the prescribed lambda; coefficients average to those in `eq:final-constants` (lines 971–998).
- **Decisive correction transition — explained in the text.** A three-point cluster inevitably contributes pairs. Thus setting its third density to q_3 requires reserving its pair marginal before adding two-point clusters: F_3=q_3 and F_2=q_2−M q_3 (`eq:F-kernels`, lines 716–728). The marginal bound and added delta/D term make F_2 nonnegative, even for thin triangles. The ordered-selection calculation gives six triples and six ordered pairs from a triple, while a pair gives two ordered pairs; the factors in the cluster probabilities cancel these counts (lines 901–917). This explains why the resulting second density is F_2+M F_3=q_2, rather than an independently assigned deficit. I did not need to supply a missing argument here.

Substantive repair directions, in priority order:

1. **Make rejection-estimator normalization explicit.** Section 5, “Correction-cluster marks,” lines 1155–1172, says each accepted row contributes T H_3j or Q_2 H_2j, but does not state whether the mean divides by all proposals or only accepted rows. The distinction changes the answer. For a triple proposal with density E/T and acceptance q_3/E, the intended identity is `E[T 1_accept H_3j] = integral q_3 H_3j`; therefore the estimator is the sum of these contributions divided by the total number of proposals, with rejected proposals contributing zero. Accepted-only averaging would instead introduce the factor T/Q_3. The pair estimator has the analogous issue. Add one sentence or one displayed estimator giving this denominator and zero-rejection convention, and say the same observations enter variance calculations.
