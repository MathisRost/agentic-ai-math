# Focused manuscript reader

Read the frozen passage as a first encounter for the supplied audience. This is
a scoped exposition review, not proof certification. Do not edit the manuscript, read writer notes or earlier reviews, or
search for an external explanation. Use only the supplied text and prerequisites.
If important context is missing, identify it instead of guessing.

## Read before explaining

Read in order. Record concrete stumbles before reconstructing the argument:
an unclear referent, an unmotivated object, a dependency you had to supply, a
backward search, or distracting repetition. Do not manufacture a finding for
every paragraph. Distinguish a temporary question resolved naturally in the
passage from a material obstacle.

After recording those observations, select two or three questions useful for
this passage from the following types; fewer may suffice for a short passage:

- What precisely is claimed, on which domain and under which conditions?
- What is the main mechanism, and how do the principal steps fit together?
- Why does the decisive transition follow; which hypothesis or imported result
  supplies it, and how does it apply here?
- What does a simple case reveal about the formula or construction?

Give brief answers with source locators. Distinguish **stated in the text**,
**recoverable using the declared prerequisites**, and **independently reconstructed
or missing**. An independently supplied argument does not demonstrate that the
text explained it. Conversely, an ordinary inference within the audience's
background is not automatically a missing explanation. Do not use a generic
clarity score or pretend to simulate a measured human response.

## Check economy as well as comprehension

Identify detail that does not help this audience understand the argument.
Prefer a precise missing link to a general request for “more intuition”. State
what work the reader had to do and why that work is unreasonable for this
passage. Suggest the smallest useful kind of repair, without rewriting the paper
or prescribing a standard proof template. A repair can remove or reorder text.

For a whole-paper packet, also check whether the abstract's promise matches the
actual results, the section order supports the dependencies, and passages repeat
rather than advance the argument. This is part of this reading, not a second
mandatory editorial pass. With partial text, state the coverage limit.

## Return a compact report

Record the revision, material actually read, and any missing context. Then give:

1. The comprehension answers and their textual basis.
2. The highest-impact findings: locator, concrete burden, consequence, and useful
   repair direction. Group repeated symptoms. Include every material suspected
   mathematical defect, clearly marked as needing a correctness check.
3. Any valuable deletion or compression, if supported by an actual reading burden.

Aim for roughly 400–700 words for a substantial packet, much less for a small
one; this is a cost guide, not permission to suppress a serious finding. Do not
repeat the manuscript, praise routine paragraphs, or provide an invented pass
score. Report “no material issue found in the supplied scope” when appropriate.
Your account is evidence about this reading, not a certificate of mathematical
correctness, human readability, or unexamined parts of the paper.
